# BREACHLINE

Co-op tactical entry game for Quest (WebXR), phones, tablets and desktop, up to 4 officers. Everyone arrives at a training facility (shooting range with static, pop-up and moving targets, a timed shoot / no-shoot kill house, and practice doors and a dummy bomb that reset themselves); the mission board there picks one of four maps (mansion, container terminal, Grand Prix pit building with the race still running: stay off the track, and an airport: kerbside canopy, double-height departures hall with a glass gallery, security lanes, airside concourse, then across the apron to a maintenance hangar where a grounded jet holds a hostage, a gunman and a bomb in its cargo hold; the high-value target starts in the upstairs VIP lounge and runs for the jet as soon as he hears the squad), the weather and, for the Grand Prix and the airport, day or night, and START sends the whole room in together. PvP: anyone can switch TEAM to TERRORIST on the board; with at least one terrorist the next mission is a PvP round (terrorist players start inside, allied with the full AI crew, can't rescue or defuse; SWAT wins by securing every hostage and bomb or taking out every terrorist, terrorists win when the timer runs out or every officer is down; one life, downed players spectate a teammate). A terrorist player can take a hostage as a human shield: free-hand grip on them in VR (USE on phone/desktop), released by letting go; the hostage covers most of the body, head and right side stay exposed; half speed and pistol only while holding; a SWAT hit or a flashbang frees the hostage, and a held hostage can't be cuffed. SWAT bots: missions fill the squad to four with AI officers (ALPHA, BRAVO, CHARLIE; three when you play alone, one fewer for each friend, and a friend who joins mid-mission takes a bot's place). They follow you, shout down anyone who hasn't complied, and shoot suspects who open fire or threaten a hostage (never an unaware or surrendered one). Point at something and open the order wheel: VR hold Y/B (a tap still shouts), desktop hold G and move the mouse toward an order, phone SQUAD. Orders: FOLLOW ME, HOLD, MOVE THERE (at a spot), OPEN / PICK LOCK / KICK / BREACH & CLEAR / CUT WIRE (at a door; breach kicks or opens, throws a flashbang and clears the room), DEFUSE (at a bomb), DETAIN (at a hostage or surrendered suspect), REVIVE (at a downed officer; they also revive you unasked). Bots check doors for wires and spot most of them. With nobody on the SWAT side (a solo terrorist) the bots run the mission themselves. Terrorist players also carry 2 door locks and 2 wire traps: with the LOCK (lockpick slot) or TRAP (cutters slot) tool, hold USE / grip on a closed door (2 s to lock, 3 s to rig); terrorists pass their own locked and trapped doors, SWAT has to pick or kick the locks and trips the wires unless they spot and disarm them. Home-screen installs check for a new build each time they come back to the front and reload when not mid-mission. After a mission, TRAINING on the debrief returns to the range; suspects, hostages and bombs are reshuffled every mission. Dusk, with a breaker that cuts the lights, and night vision.
- **Mansion** (Operation GILDED CAGE): a three-storey modern house, walk-out basement and pool terrace above the ocean. 9 suspects, 5 hostages, 2 bombs, 12:00.
- **Terminal** (Operation IRON TIDE): a container port about 120 x 110 m. Container yard, ship-to-shore and yard cranes, trucks, an operations building (breaker), and a moored container ship: board by a gangway, cross the stacked deck, clear the bridge tower. 14 suspects, 8 hostages, 3 bombs in padlocked containers (cut the lock with the cutters, open the doors, defuse inside), 15:00. A high-value target with two bodyguards holds the bridge: arrest him for full marks.

## Deploy (GitHub + one Cloudflare Worker)
1. Cloudflare dashboard > Workers & Pages > Create > Import a repository > pick this repo.
2. Deploy command: `npx wrangler deploy` (the default). No build command, no variables.
3. Open the `*.workers.dev` URL in the Quest browser, enter a room code, press ENTER VR. Friends use the same code.

`wrangler.toml` serves `/public` as static assets and routes `/ws/<ROOM>` to a Durable Object (`Room`) that runs the mission.

## Layout
- `public/js/core/` pure game logic shared by server and client: `level.js` (walls, collision, bullets, pathfinding for any map), `maps/` (one file per map: layout, floors, spawn points), `sim.js` (suspects, hostages, bombs, rules of engagement, scoring), `weapon.js` (manual gun handling), `host.js` (message protocol).
- `public/js/` client: `game.js` (VR rig, hands, weapons, HUD, networking), `gfx.js` (shared world rendering), `maps/<id>-gfx.js` (each map's scenery and sky), `actors.js` (people, guns, bombs), `audio.js`.
- `src/worker.js` Worker + Durable Object.
- `test/` `node test/core.test.mjs`, `node test/worker.test.mjs`; `vr.py` + `xrmock.js` drive the VR path in headless Chromium with a fake headset.

## Art and sound
People, weapons, furniture and the house are built in code; the race car is "Ferrari F1 2019" by valvetin (CC BY 4.0), stripped to solid colours with no logos (`public/models/f1`, see its LICENSE.md; the Kerbside car in `js/racecar.js` stands in until it loads). Downloaded models: suspects and the squad (`public/models/chars`, see its LICENSE.md; two are derived from Valve's Counter-Strike characters, keep the project private or swap them before publishing), the officers' rifle and pistol (`public/models/m4`: "Assault rifle M4A1" by bobeer, CC BY 4.0; "FN509 Pistol" by Lokeig, CC BY-NC 4.0, non-commercial), civilians and off-duty police hostages (`public/models/civ`, by Michael Constantine, CC BY 4.0), plants (`public/models/nature`, CC0) and the parked car (`public/models/car`, CC-BY 4.0, Car Concept by Eric Chadwick / Darmstadt Graphics Group, simplified); see the LICENSE.md in each folder. Materials come from `public/js/mats.js` (Phong shine plus a tiny reflection map, same approach as Kerbside).
Sound effects are recorded CC0 libraries from GitHub (Free Firearm Sound Library and Kenney packs), built by `tools/build_sfx.py`; credits in `public/sfx/CREDITS.md`.
