Common questions

I bought the full game but missions are still locked

Make sure the headset is online and signed in to the Meta account that made the purchase, then close the game fully and open it again: the unlock is checked every time the game starts. It carries over to any headset signed in to that account.

Voice chat doesn't work

Press VOICE CHAT on the start menu and allow the microphone when asked. If you said no earlier, open Settings > Privacy & safety > App permissions > Microphone on the headset and turn it on for You Can't Hide.

How do I play with friends?

Choose PLAY ONLINE, press NEW to get a 4-digit room code and share it. Friends enter the same code. Up to 4 players per room. Online play is part of the full game.

How do I report another player?

Press the Meta button on your right controller and choose Report. Meta passes the report to us.

I lost connection during a mission

The game rejoins the room by itself, and the mission carries on. If it says the room is gone, start a new room from PLAY ONLINE.

Refunds

Purchases are made through the Meta Horizon Store, so refunds are handled by Meta under its refund policy: see your purchase history in the Meta Horizon app.

How do I learn the controls?

Play the TUTORIAL from the start menu (about ten minutes), or read How to play.
