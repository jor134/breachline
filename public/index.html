BREACHLINE

Co-op tactical entry for Quest · up to 4 officers
Solo practice (no server)
Gun tilt
Controls

VR: left stick walk (click = jog), right stick snap turn. Grip near chest = rifle, right hip = pistol, left belt = magazine. Grip again to stow. Trigger fires. Gun hand A/X drops the mag, B/Y releases the bolt, stick click = semi/auto. Pull the charging handle / slide back with your other hand to rack. Grab the handguard with your other hand to steady the rifle. Other hand B/Y = shout for compliance; tap A/X = flashlight, hold A/X = tool wheel (lockpick, wire cutters, hands, night vision); stick click = kick a door. Grip at your helmet = night vision. Grip the radio on your left chest = menu. Hold grip on a kneeling person to cuff. Cut bomb wires with the trigger of an empty hand.

Desktop: WASD, mouse, left click fire, right click aim, R reload, F shout, E use / cuff / cut, V semi/auto, 1/2 weapon, 3/4/5 hands/lockpick/cutters, N night vision, Q kick, M menu, C crouch, T flashlight, Enter start (dusk), \ start (night).
