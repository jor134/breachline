BREACHLINE
Co-op tactical entry · Quest, phone, tablet or desktop · up to 4 officers

  
  
  
Solo practice (no server)

  
Gun tilt

  

  
  
  
  

  
Controls
  
VR: left stick walk (click = jog), right stick snap turn. Grip near chest = rifle, right hip = pistol, left belt = magazine. Grip again to stow. Trigger fires. Gun hand A/X drops the mag, B/Y releases the bolt, stick click = semi/auto. Pull the charging handle / slide back with your other hand to rack. Grab the handguard with your other hand to steady the rifle. Other hand B/Y = shout for compliance; tap A/X = flashlight, hold A/X = tool wheel (lockpick, wire cutters, door camera, med kit, hands, night vision). Flashbangs and smoke are on the front of your vest: grip one, swing, let go to throw; stick click = kick a door. Grip at your helmet = night vision. Grip the radio on your left chest = menu. Hold grip on a kneeling person to cuff. Cut bomb wires with the trigger of an empty hand.

  
Phone / tablet: hold the device sideways. Left thumb moves, drag anywhere else to look. USE opens doors, cuffs, picks locks (TOOL set to PICK) and cuts wires (TOOL set to CUTTER).

  
Desktop: WASD, mouse, left click fire, right click aim, R reload, F shout, E use / cuff / cut, V semi/auto, 1/2 weapon, 3/4/5/8 hands/lockpick/cutters/door camera, 6 flashbang, 7 smoke, H med kit, N night vision, Q kick, M menu, C crouch, T flashlight, O weather choice, Enter start, L end mission. Walking out of the front gate also ends it. E at the main breaker (plant room, basement) cuts the power.
