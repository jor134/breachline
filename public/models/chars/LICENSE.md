# Character models

All of these were downloaded from Sketchfab, where they are listed under CC BY 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Changes for the game: textures re-encoded to JPEG (PNG where they need transparency), the bundled animation removed,
old specular-glossiness materials converted to metal-roughness (tools/pack_glb.py). In game they are posed by code (js/chars.js).

- `thug.glb`: "terrorist" by DJMaesen (https://sketchfab.com/bumstrum), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/terrorist-ccce1abd9086451da3bed9ec30c82c37
- `gunman.glb`: "Terrorist 4" by Upgrade titan camera man (https://sketchfab.com/Upgradetitancameraman), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/terrorist-4-5c7aa149588f418eacb72adc9beea1ec
- `phoenix.glb`: "Tm Phoenix V2- csgo terrorist characters" by ErhanMatur (https://sketchfab.com/erhanmatur), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tm-phoenix-v2-csgo-terrorist-characters-8cd9781067b24cf4b36f0f2916f9ac35
- `boss.glb`: "Business Man" by qsardor (https://sketchfab.com/qsardor57913), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/business-man-71abfba65139471dabac10ffeb5bf2bb
- `swat.glb`: "S.W.A.T. Operator" by Mateusz Woliński, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/swat-operator-9e82fabf26194896b5ad4a364d864eab (simplified to about 31k triangles with textures at 512 px by tools/rig_glb.py; the face, mask, gloves, uniform and patches kept at full detail)

Note: `phoenix.glb` is derived from Valve's Counter-Strike characters (Counter-Strike is a trademark of Valve Corporation).
The CC BY listing on Sketchfab does not come from Valve, so it is not covered by a licence Valve granted. Keep this project private / non-commercial, or replace it with an original model before publishing.
