# Grand Prix circuit

**spa.blx**: built by tools/spa_kit.py from
- "Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps 1992 layout" by Dave Love (Sketchfab, Tyler_Dave):
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/circuit-de-spa-francorchamps-1992-layout-a1223a21df954b9ebf8eb3b7c682eb99 (CC BY 4.0)

Changes: placed around the game's own pit building and paddock, which are cut out of it; the ground levelled along the pit
straight; everything beyond ~650 m and the small detail beyond ~350 m removed; textures shrunk; the DRS boards and the circuit's pit-wall rails along the paddock removed; its buildings taken out and
rebuilt by the game from their footprints.
The game shows no real circuit, team or sponsor names.
