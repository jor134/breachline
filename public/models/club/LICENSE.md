# Club models

**kit.blx** — "Night Club" by MBRODERI2 (https://sketchfab.com/MBRODERI2)
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/night-club-51698a2a7171450ea40438a5dbc105f5 — License: CC BY 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
Modified by tools/club_kit.py: cut into furniture kits (bar, shelves, booths, stools, DJ stand, speakers, screen, lights, dance floor),
re-centred, materials converted from specular-glossiness to metal-roughness, textures shrunk.

textures packed into one per person, written as morph targets.
