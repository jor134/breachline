# Soccer stadium shell (stadium.blx)

"Modern Stadium" by Razny (https://sketchfab.com/razniak1910)
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/modern-stadium-21767e058e9e4df8a1c57352611756aa
License: Sketchfab Standard (https://sketchfab.com/licenses). Do not redistribute the model on its own.

Modified for BREACHLINE by tools/stadium_glb.py: turned and rescaled into the game's frame, the main stand's box level and the roof
over it cut out for the playable VIP block, the pitch and railings removed, the seats rebuilt as row strips, every other material
simplified and flat-coloured.

Shipped obfuscated (tools/obf_glb.py, original in assets-src/, not served) so the model is never available as a stand-alone file.
