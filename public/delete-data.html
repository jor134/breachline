YOU CAN'T HIDE · DELETE YOUR DATA

Brecciastudio · Privacy policy

In the game (instant)

  1. Open You Can't Hide on your Meta Quest.
  2. On the start screen, choose CREDITS, then PRIVACY & MY DATA.
  3. Choose DELETE ALL MY DATA, then YES, DELETE ALL MY DATA.

This deletes your friends account on our server (callsign, last time online, current room, friends, friend requests and blocks) and everything the game saved on your headset. It cannot be undone; your purchase of the full game is kept by Meta and is not affected.

By email (anything else, or if you no longer have the game)

Email jor134@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Include your callsign (shown on the game's start screen) and, for a shared map, its 8-digit code. We delete the data we hold about you (friends account, shared maps, player reports, server logs) and confirm by email within 30 days.

What is never stored

Online room data, positions in the game and voice chat are never stored, so there is nothing of them to delete.
