BREACHLINE

How to playBack to the game

You lead a SWAT squad. Get in, rescue the hostages, arrest the suspects and defuse the bomb before the timer runs out. You can shoot anyone, but you're scored on what's justified: shoot only people who threaten you.

START THE HANDS-ON TUTORIAL It runs solo, about ten minutes, then takes you on a first mission. You can also start it from the TUTORIAL button on the start screen or on the mission board in the training facility.

Getting started

  1. Install it (optional): on the start screen tap Install as app, or use your browser's "Add to Home Screen". On a Quest, open the game in the Quest browser.
  2. Type a callsign (your name over the radio).
  3. Solo: tick Solo practice. Three squad bots join you. With friends: untick it and everyone types the same room code.
  4. Pick how you play: ENTER VR (Quest), PLAY ON THIS PHONE / TABLET (hold it sideways) or Desktop (keyboard and mouse).
  5. Everyone arrives at the training facility. Walk to the mission board in the lobby, choose a mission and press START MISSION.
In VR, check Gun tilt on the start screen if your rifle points too high or low in your hand. If your body or vest looks wrong when you look down, press BODY FIT on the mission board.

The mission

  • Objectives: bombs defused, hostages secured (cuffed and safe), suspects arrested. The timer is on your wrist (VR) or at the top of the screen.
  • Shout first. Hostages kneel when you shout. Suspects may give up, run, or fight; every shout adds pressure, and a flashbanged suspect gives up far more easily.
  • Cuff anyone kneeling with their hands up, hostages included. Watch surrendered suspects: some fake it.
  • When to shoot: a suspect who is armed and threatening you or a hostage. Shooting someone unarmed or surrendered, or a civilian, costs points; so does friendly fire.
  • Bombs: the bomb shows which wire colours to cut and in what order. A wrong wire takes 45 seconds off the clock.
  • Lights out: the main breaker cuts all the lights. Suspects can't see; you can with night vision. The mission board says where it is.
  • Finish: walk out of the front gate or use END MISSION on the menu. The report lists everything you scored and lost, with a grade.
  • Down? A teammate or bot can revive you.

Squad bots and orders

Playing with fewer than four people, bots fill the empty SWAT slots (a friend who joins takes a bot's place). They follow you and fight back on their own. Give orders with the squad wheel (VR: hold B/Y and point; phone: SQUAD; desktop: G):

Follow / HoldStay on you, or hold where they stand.

Open / Pick / KickOpen the door, pick its lock quietly, or kick it in.

Tools and doors

  • Lockpick: quiet way through a locked door. Kick: fast and loud.
  • Door camera: look under a door before you open it.
  • Rigged doors: cut the wire with the wire cutters before opening.
  • Flashbangs (2) stun everyone who sees the flash; smoke (1) hides you.
  • Med kit: heals you once per mission.

Setting up a game

Everything is chosen on the mission board in the training facility lobby (VR and desktop: walk to it; phone: the buttons along the bottom).

BODY FIT (VR)Line your body and vest up under the headset. Saved on the headset.

During a mission the menu (VR: radio; phone: MENU; desktop: M) can end it, restart with new positions, or go back to the home screen.

Playing with friends and voice chat

  1. Everyone untick Solo practice and type the same room code. Up to four players.
  2. Tick Voice chat on the start screen and allow the microphone. Mute: MIC on the radio menu (VR), MIC button (phone), B (desktop).
  3. Any player can pick the mission and start it from the board.
If someone's connection drops, they reload the page and join the same room code again.

PvP: SWAT vs terrorists

Press TEAM on the board to play as a terrorist. If anyone is a terrorist, the next mission is PvP: terrorists start inside with the AI suspects and hostages, SWAT breach. Playing a terrorist solo pits you against the three squad bots.

The training facility

  • Range: static, pop-up and moving targets. Your hits and accuracy show on the board.
  • Kill house: four rooms against the clock. Shoot red (armed), never blue (no-shoot): each blue hit adds 5 seconds.
  • Skill bay: a locked door to pick, a rigged door, a door to kick and a dummy bomb.

Level editor

The LEVEL EDITOR link on the start screen builds maps on a phone or computer: rooms and floors, doors and windows, stairs, furniture, sculpted ground, trees, shrubs, walls, fences and gates, plus where suspects, hostages and bombs go. Check finds problems, Test plays your draft solo, Publish (with the creator code) puts it on everyone's mission board. Delete published maps from Open → Published.

Tips

  • Slow is smooth. Stop at every door, look under it or crack it, then go in with the squad behind you.
  • Flash, then shout: stunned suspects give up.
  • Count your magazines. Reload behind cover, not in the doorway.
  • Hostages first: a suspect may execute hostages if things go loud.
  • Cuff surrendered suspects quickly; some fake it and reach for a gun.
