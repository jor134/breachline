BREACHLINE PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 17 November 2026

BREACHLINE is a virtual reality game for Meta Quest made by [DEVELOPER LEGAL NAME], an independent developer in Toronto, Canada ("we"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, and how to have it deleted. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect and why

  • Your callsign and room code. When you play online, the callsign you pick and the room code are sent to our game server so the other players in your room can see your name and so you join the right room. The server keeps them only in memory while the room is open; nothing is saved after everyone leaves.
  • Your position and actions in the game (where your head, hands and weapons are, shots, doors, orders) are sent to the game server during online play so everyone sees the same mission. They are not stored.
  • Voice chat. If you turn voice chat on, your microphone audio goes directly to the other players in your room. When a direct connection isn't possible it is relayed through Cloudflare's TURN service. We do not record, store or listen to voice chat. You can turn your microphone off, or mute any other player, from the in-game menu.
  • Technical data. Our server runs on Cloudflare. Like any internet service, Cloudflare processes your IP address and request details to deliver the game, and our server's request logs (which include the room code and callsign in the connection address) are kept by Cloudflare for a short period to help us fix problems. We do not use them to identify or track you.
  • Settings saved on your headset (callsign, voice on or off, body fit, tutorial progress, map editor drafts) stay in the game's storage on your device. They are not sent to us.
  • Purchases. Buying the full game is handled by Meta through the Meta Horizon Store. We never see your payment details. The game asks the store only whether the full game has been purchased on your account.

We do not sell personal data, show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, or share data with anyone except the service providers named above (Cloudflare for hosting and voice relay, Meta for the store and purchases).

Reports about other players

If you report a player through the Meta Quest menu (Meta button > Report), Meta forwards the report to us in encrypted form. We use it only to look into the behaviour reported and keep it no longer than needed for that.

Children

BREACHLINE is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Deleting your data

  • Data saved on your headset is deleted when you uninstall the game, or by clearing the app's data in the headset settings.
  • We keep no account or profile for you. To ask us to delete anything else we might hold, such as a player report or server logs, email [CONTACT EMAIL] with the callsign and approximate date you played. We will reply within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA or the EU/UK GDPR) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to complain to a data protection authority. Email us to use these rights.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the game's update notes.
