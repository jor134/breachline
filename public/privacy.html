2. Who processes it

  • Cloudflare runs our game server, storage and voice relay, so data may be processed in data centres outside Canada, including the United States.
  • Meta handles the store, payments and purchase checks.

Both process the data only to provide their service to us.

3. Deleting your data

  • In the game: CREDITS > PRIVACY & MY DATA > DELETE ALL MY DATA (press twice). This deletes your friends account on our server (callsign, last online, room, friends, requests and blocks, both ways) and everything the game saved on your headset. You can also delete only your friends data in START MISSION > JOIN A SQUAD > FRIENDS > DELETE MY FRIENDS DATA.
  • By email or the web: for anything else (a shared map, a player report, server logs), or if you no longer have the game, follow youcanthidegame.com/delete-data or email jor134@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and your callsign (and the map code, if it is about a shared map). We delete it and confirm within 30 days.
  • On your headset: uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in the headset settings, also deletes everything saved there.

Room data, gameplay data and voice are never stored, so there is nothing of them to delete.

4. Children

You Can't Hide is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used the game, email us and we will delete any data linked to them.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA, or the EU or UK GDPR) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to complain to a data protection authority. Email jor134@gmail.com to use these rights; we reply within 30 days.

6. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the game's update notes.
